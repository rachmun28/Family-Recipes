=== VIA Rail Canada's Plum Pudding
cat: Desserts
serves: 12-16
from: VIA Rail Canada, adapted from Bill Nellis's 1930s recipe (Toronto Star)
scan: Plum Pudding and Hot Brandy Sauce.jpg
--- Pudding
- 1 cup Thompson raisins
- 1 cup sultana raisins
- 1 cup currants
- 2 oz dark rum
- 2 oz stout (such as Guinness)
- juice and finely grated zest of 1 lemon
- juice and finely grated zest of 1 orange
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/4 tsp almond extract
- 2 large eggs
- 1 Granny Smith apple, peeled and finely chopped
- 2/3 cup chopped mixed peel
- 1 cup frozen chopped beef suet, thawed
- 1/2 cup finely chopped walnuts
- 1/2 cup finely chopped almonds
- 1 1/2 cups dried breadcrumbs
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
- 1/2 tsp each baking soda and ground cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp each ground ginger, nutmeg, allspice and salt
* steam 5 min over simmering water to soften; move to a bowl or bag | 1-3
> add; cover; stand at room temperature at least 12 hrs, stirring | 4-9
* beat on high until soft peaks | 10
> stir in | 11 12
> add raisin mixture; mix well | s2
* stir together | 13-20
* stir into egg mixture (8–10 cups) | s5 s6
> pack into a greased heatproof mould or bowl; tie a tea towel over (or use four 19-oz cans, 1 inch headspace, parchment and foil)
> stand upright in a tall saucepan; fill ¾ up the sides with water; boil, then simmer covered — 5 hrs for a large pudding, 4 for small
> cool on a rack; stand 30 min before unmoulding (or cool, cover and refrigerate)
--- Hot brandy sauce
- 2 cups packed brown sugar
- 1/4 cup unsalted butter
- 2 cups water
- grated zest of 1 lemon
- 2 tbsp cornstarch dissolved in 2 tbsp cold water
- 2 oz brandy
* cook over medium-high until the sugar melts | 1-4
> stir in; stir until thick | 5
> stir in | 6
--- Serve
* slice or crumble pudding; serve with sauce | p1 p2
note: Reheat in the microwave or back in the mould in a water bath. Tradition: add coins to the mixture (warn guests!), or flame it with hot rum or whisky at the table.

=== Pork & Apple Sauté
cat: Pork
serves: 4
scan: Pork Apple Saute.jpg
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 lb boneless pork loin chops, ¾ inch thick, fat trimmed
- salt and black pepper
- 1/2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 firm unpeeled apple, in 1/4-inch wedges
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1/2 cup chicken broth
- 1 tsp grainy Dijon mustard
- 1/2 tsp thyme
* heat in a large frying pan over medium-high | 1
> season pork and sauté ~4 min per side until golden and cooked; keep warm | 2 3
> reduce to medium; add oil if needed | 4
> add; cook, stirring, ~1 min until golden | 5 6
> add, scraping up the bits | 7-9
> boil, then simmer until apples are tender, ~3 min; season
> return pork and juices; heat through
> serve pork with apples and sauce drizzled over

=== Pork with Maple Glaze
cat: Pork
serves: 4
scan: Pork with Maple Glaze.jpg
- 2 tbsp oil
- 2 shallots, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh gingerroot, minced
- 1/3 cup rice vinegar (or 1/4 cup white vinegar)
- 1/4 cup apple juice
- 1 lb pork filet
- 3 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1/4 cup apple juice
- few drops Tabasco (optional)
- salt and pepper, to taste
* heat in a pan | 1
> sauté | 2-4
> add; mix well | 5 6
> pour over meat in a casserole or bag; marinate 1–2 hrs in the fridge, turning | 7
* mix; set aside | 8-12
* fold in the small end of the filet; barbecue covered, 5 inches from the heat, ~10 min, turning and basting | s4 s5
> it's done at 160°F inside
> serve with egg noodles and boiled turnip
note: Pork tastes better served still a bit pink.
