=== Brown Sugar Shortbread Fans
cat: Cookies
serves: 4 dozen
from: Gay Lea
scan: Shortbread Toffee and Brown Sugar.jpg
bake: 300F 20 sheet
pre: Preheat oven to 300°F
pre: Line cookie sheets with parchment
- 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
- 3/4 tsp salt
- 3/4 cup packed light brown sugar
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
* beat until light & fluffy | 1-3
> stir in, ⅓ cup at a time | 4
> divide in sixths; pat each into a 6-inch circle (two per sheet)
> press fork tines or a knife tip around the edges; score each into 8 wedges
> {bake} in the centre, until golden
> cool 5 min; cut through the score marks; cool completely
note: Perfect for a shortbread mould, too.
