=== Lemon Blast Bombe
cat: Desserts
serves: 16
from: Chef Cathy Goldman, Succotash Catering (Toronto Star)
scan: Lemon Blast Bombe pg 1.jpg
--- Lemon curd
- 4 lemons
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- 8 eggs, lightly beaten
- 2 egg yolks
- 1 cup unsalted butter, in cubes
* finely grate peel from 2 lemons; squeeze 1 cup juice | 1
> whisk with sugar, eggs and yolks in an 8-cup microwave-safe bowl | 2-4
> add | 5
> cover with plastic, leaving a gap; microwave on medium-high 3 min; whisk well
> repeat about 4 more times until thick and it coats a spoon; strain if needed; stir in the peel
> press waxed paper on the surface; refrigerate until cold, at least 2 hrs (up to 3 days)
--- Sponge cake
- 7 egg yolks
- 7 egg whites
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 3 tbsp water or orange juice
- 1 cup cake-and-pastry flour
- 1/2 tsp cream of tartar
- 1/4 tsp salt
* beat yolks with ⅔ cup of the sugar and the vanilla on high until thick, ~4 min | 1 3 4
> beat in; scrape into a large bowl | 5
> sift flour over the top — don't mix | 6
* beat with cream of tartar and salt until foamy; add the rest of the sugar a tablespoon at a time to stiff peaks | 2 7 8
* stir ¼ of the whites into the yolks, then fold in the rest just until no streaks | s3 s4
> spread in an ungreased 12 × 17-inch jelly roll pan
> bake in the centre at 325°F 15 min, until it springs back
--- Whipped cream & assembly
- 3 cups whipping cream
- 1/4 cup sifted icing sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla
* line a bowl with the cake; fill with curd | p1 p2
* whip until stiff | 1-3
* finish with cream (see note) | s1 s2
note: Only the first page of this clipping was scanned, so the steps for lining the bowl, layering and unmoulding are missing. The cake is used as a shell for the curd and lightened cream, then refrigerated about 8 hours.

=== Hilton Harbour Castle Lemon Chiffon Pie
