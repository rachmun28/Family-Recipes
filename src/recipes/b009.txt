=== Chicken Véronique
cat: Chicken & Poultry
serves: 4-6
scan: Chicken Veronique pg 1.jpg
scan: Chicken Veronique pg 2.jpg
pre: Preheat oven to 375°F
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 1/2 lb chicken pieces
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/4 cup marmalade
- 1 cup chicken stock
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 3 tbsp lemon juice
- 1/4 cup marmalade
- 1/2 cup seedless grapes
- lemon slices and Italian parsley, to garnish
* dredge chicken in flour with salt | 1-3
* melt in a 9 × 13 baking dish in the 375°F oven | 4 5
> arrange chicken skin side down; bake 20 min | s1
> turn chicken; bake 10 min
> brush with half the marmalade | 6
> bake 10–15 min until golden brown and tender; keep warm on a platter
> drain 2 tbsp drippings into a saucepan; add stock | 7
* blend | 8 9
* stir into stock; cook over medium heat 3–4 min until thick & clear | s7 s8
> blend in remaining marmalade; adjust seasoning | 10
> add grapes; heat through | 11
> pour sauce over chicken; garnish | 12
