=== Chicken Breasts: 10 Quick Ways
cat: Chicken & Poultry
serves: 4
scan: Chicken Breasts 10 Quick Ways.jpg
method: Each version uses 4 boned, skinned chicken breasts. Unless noted, start by sautéing them in 2 tbsp butter until golden on both sides.
method: 1. Mustard dill — add ¼ cup white wine and 2 tbsp chopped fresh dill; cover and simmer 10 min. Remove chicken; whisk 1 tbsp Dijon and ½ cup sour cream into the sauce.
method: 2. Easy piccata — coat breasts with flour, then sauté. Squeeze the juice of ½ lemon over; sprinkle with tarragon, salt and pepper; sauté gently 10 min.
method: 3. Schnitzel — dip in 1 egg whisked with 1 tbsp milk, then in ½ cup fine bread crumbs, ½ tsp Italian seasoning, ½ tsp basil, salt and pepper. Sauté in 2 tbsp butter 15 min.
method: 4. Apricot ginger — add 10 oz apricot nectar, 2 tbsp finely chopped fresh ginger and a pinch of salt; cover and simmer 10 min. Remove chicken and ginger; boil the sauce until thick enough to coat the chicken.
method: 5. Herbed Parmesan — add ¼ cup white wine and ¼ tsp tarragon; cover and simmer 10 min. Remove chicken. Add ½ cup table cream and ⅓ cup freshly grated Parmesan; boil gently, stirring constantly, until thickened.
method: 6. Orange honey — add the juice of 1 orange, 1 tbsp liquid honey and ¼ tsp each ground ginger and curry; cover and simmer 10 min.
method: 7. Hazelnut butter — add 2 tbsp white wine; cover and simmer 10 min. Remove chicken; add ¼ cup chopped hazelnuts and cook over medium heat, stirring, until most of the juices evaporate. Salt to taste.
method: 8. Roquefort — add ¼ cup water; cover and simmer 10 min. Remove chicken; add ¼ cup crumbled Roquefort and 2 finely chopped green onions; stir over medium heat until thick.
method: 9. Stroganoff — add 3 tbsp dry sherry; cover and simmer 10 min. Remove chicken; whisk in ½ cup sour cream, a dash of Worcestershire and a pinch of salt.
method: 10. Light lime — add 2 tbsp syrup from preserved ginger, 1 tbsp finely chopped preserved ginger and the juice of 1 lime; cover and simmer 10 min. Season with salt and pepper.
