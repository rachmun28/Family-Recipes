=== Escalopes de Veau Madère (Veal or Chicken Madeira)
cat: Veal
serves: 12
scan: Veal or Chicken Madeira.jpg
- 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 3/4 tsp ground pepper
- 4 lb veal scallopini
- 1/4-1/2 cup lightly salted butter or margarine
- 1/2 cup Madeira wine
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 tbsp lemon juice, preferably fresh
- 1/2 tsp minced garlic
- 3 tbsp lightly salted butter or margarine
- 1 lb fresh mushrooms, sliced
- cherry tomatoes and parsley sprigs, to garnish
* mix; dredge scallopini; shake off excess | 1-4
* heat ¼ cup in a large skillet over moderately low | 5
> brown 3 or 4 at a time, 2–3 min per side, adding butter as needed; arrange in a 13 × 9 × 2-inch dish | s1
> drain the fat; add; cook, stirring and scraping up the brown bits | 6 7
> add; cook 2–3 min until syrupy | 8 9
> pour over scallopini
> melt butter in the skillet; add mushrooms; cook 5–7 min until soft and dry | 10 11
> spoon over; cover tightly with foil (refrigerate up to 24 hrs if you like)
> put in a cold oven; turn on to 350°F; bake 40–50 min until very hot
> garnish just before serving | 12
note: Chicken version (Escalopes de Volaille Madère): use 4 lb boned, skinned chicken breasts. Loosen the two small fillets, fold them back, cut each breast in half, pound to ⅛ inch between waxed paper and cut each half into 2 small scallopini. Then proceed as above.
