=== Lemon Meringue Pie
cat: Pies & Tarts
serves: 6
scan: Lemon Meringue Pie.jpg
pre: Preheat oven to 425°F
--- Meringue
- 3 egg whites
- 1/4 tsp fresh lemon juice or cream of tartar
- 1/4 cup sugar
* beat in a large bowl until frothy | 1 2
> gradually beat in to soft peaks, then continue to stiff peaks; set aside | 3
--- Filling
- 1 1/4 cups sugar
- 6 tbsp cornstarch
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 2 cups water
- 3 egg yolks, slightly beaten
- 1/2 cup fresh lemon juice
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 baked and cooled 9-inch pie shell
* combine in a saucepan | 1-3
> stir in gradually | 4
> cook over medium, stirring, until it boils and thickens; remove from heat
> stir a little into the yolks, then return all to the pan | 5
> bring to a boil, stirring; remove from heat
> stir in | 6 7
> fill the pie shell | 8
--- Finish
* spread meringue over the hot filling right to the crust edge | p2 p1
> bake 4–5 min until golden
> let stand several hours to cool before serving
note: For the most volume, beaters and bowl must be spotlessly clean and free of fat — and keep every trace of yolk out of the whites.
