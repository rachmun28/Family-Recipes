=== Shortbread Cookie Variations
cat: Cookies
scan: Lemon Cookies2.jpg
method: These variations use a basic shortbread dough (see Basic Shortbread).
method: Brown sugar fingers — use ½ cup brown sugar instead of white. Roll into a ½-inch-thick 5 × 12-inch rectangle, trim to make it perfect, score ¾-inch-wide strips and decorate with a fork. Bake 30 min; cut when cool.
method: Chocolate-glazed nut balls — use the basic or hazelnut dough; roll into 1-inch balls, chill, and bake 20–25 min. Dip cooled cookies in chocolate glaze, let the excess drip off, then dip in ½ cup finely chopped pistachios or pecans.
method: Citrus coins — add ¼ cup lemon or orange zest and 1 tbsp juice. Roll ¼ inch thick, cut into coins, roll the edges in sanding sugar, chill and bake 20 min.
method: Espresso — add 2 tbsp espresso powder. Roll ¼ inch thick and cut into ovals scored down the middle.
method: Chocolate-glazed fingers — cut into 1 × 3-inch rectangles, bake 20–25 min and drizzle with glaze.

=== Low-Fat Lemon Meringue Mousse
cat: Desserts
serves: about 11 cups
from: Susan Sampson, Toronto Star (adapted from Fresh Tarts)
scan: Lemon Meringue Mousse.jpg
- 4 tsp unflavoured gelatin
- 1/3 cup cold water
- 1 cup lemon juice (from 4 lemons)
- 10 large egg yolks
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 10 large egg whites
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
* sprinkle gelatin over water; let soften | 1 2
* whisk in the top of a double boiler over boiling water until thick and creamy yellow, ~5 min (don't boil) | 3-5
* whisk in 30 s until dissolved | s1 s2
> off the heat, whisk 1 min; pour into a large bowl; cool to room temperature
* beat to soft peaks on medium-high | 6
> very gradually beat in, until glossy with stiff peaks | 7
* fold gently into yolk mixture | s4 s6
> spoon into a bowl or glasses; chill overnight or until firm
note: Firm enough for a cake or jelly-roll filling. Raw egg whites aren't advised for the very young or old, pregnant or chronically ill — pasteurized whites work.
