=== Chocolate-Raspberry Torte
cat: Cakes
serves: 10
from: Regan Daley, In the Sweet Kitchen
scan: Chocolate Raspberry Torte.jpg
bake: 350F 50-60 round-9
pre: Preheat oven to 350°F
pre: Grease a 9-inch round pan, line the bottom with parchment, grease the paper and dust with flour
--- Cake
- 5 1/2 oz semi-sweet chocolate, chopped
- 3 oz unsweetened chocolate, chopped
- 2/3 cup unsalted butter, cut into small pieces
- 1/2 cup seedless raspberry jam
- 4 large eggs
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1 tbsp raspberry liqueur
- 1 tsp vanilla or raspberry extract
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 3/4 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp salt
* melt in a bowl over barely simmering water, stirring | 1-3
> when almost melted add jam; turn off heat; stir until smooth; cool slightly | 4
* beat with a mixer until thick, pale and ribbony | 5 6
> beat in | 7 8
* stir together with a fork | 9-11
* beat cooled chocolate into the egg mixture until smooth | s2 s4
> fold in | s5
> scrape into pan; smooth top
> {bake} in the centre, until cracked on top and a skewer through a crack has a few moist crumbs
> cool in pan 10 min; invert onto a rack; peel off paper; place on a platter
--- Raspberry syrup
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1 tbsp corn syrup
- 1/4 cup water
- 1/4 cup raspberry liqueur
* stir in a small heavy saucepan over medium-low until sugar dissolves | 1-3
> bring to a boil; boil 30 s without stirring
> remove from heat; cool 5 min; stir in; cool | 4
--- Serve
- about 2 cups fresh raspberries
- 1 ripe mango, peeled and diced
* brush top and sides of the warm cake with syrup | p1 p2
* stir into remaining syrup no more than 2–3 hrs before serving | 1 2
* spoon around each wedge; drizzle with syrup | s1 s2
note: The syrup can be made up to 3 days ahead. The brushed cake keeps 1 day at room temperature, 3 days refrigerated, or can be frozen.
