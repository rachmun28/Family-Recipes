=== Amish Friendship Bread
cat: Breads & Muffins
serves: 2 loaves
scan: Amish Friendship Bread.jpg
bake: 325F 50-60 loaf-9*2
pre: Starter rules: do not refrigerate, follow the instructions daily, and don't open the bag unless told to
--- 10-day starter (begin with 1 cup of starter in a zip-top bag)
- 1 cup starter (from a friend)
- 1 cup flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup milk
- 1 cup flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup milk
* days 1–5: day 1 do nothing; days 2–5 mush the bag | 1
> day 6: add, then mush the bag | 2-4
> days 7–9: let the air out & mush the bag
> day 10: pour into a bowl; add and stir | 5-7
> put 1 cup into each of 4 large zip-top bags marked "Day 1" and give away with these instructions; keep the rest
--- Bread
- 3/4 cup oil
- 3 eggs
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 large box vanilla instant pudding
- 2 cups flour
- 3/4 cup milk
- cinnamon & sugar, for the pans and tops
* add to the remaining starter | p1 1-10
* grease & flour 2 non-metal loaf pans (or 4 mini loaf pans); sprinkle cinnamon sugar on the bottoms | 11
> pour in batter; sprinkle cinnamon & sugar on top | s1
> {bake} (40 min for mini loaf pans)

=== Angel Pie with Meringue Crust
cat: Pies & Tarts
serves: 6
scan: Angel Pie with Meringue Crust.jpg
pre: Preheat oven to 275°F
pre: Grease a 9-inch pie plate
--- Meringue crust
- 3 egg whites
- pinch cream of tartar
- 1 cup granulated sugar
* beat until stiff | 1 2
> add gradually; beat 10–15 min until stiff and glossy | 3
> spread over bottom and up sides of pie plate
> bake at 275°F 1 hr, until light brown and crisp
> turn off oven, open door; let cool 15 min
--- Filling
- 113 g (4 oz) package lemon pie filling
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup cold water
- 3 egg yolks
- 2 cups hot water
- 1 tbsp butter
- 2 cups whipped topping
* combine; blend well | 1-4
> add | 5
> cook & stir over medium heat until it boils and thickens
> remove from heat; stir in | 6
> chill 1 hr
> fold in 1 cup of the topping | 7
--- Assemble
* spoon filling into cooled shell | p1 p2
> chill; garnish with remaining whipped topping

=== Apple Coffee Round
cat: Cakes
serves: about 8
from: Robin Hood
scan: Apple Coffee Cake Round.jpg
bake: 350F 45-50 springform-9
pre: Preheat oven to 350°F
pre: Grease a 9-inch springform pan
--- Topping (optional)
- 3 tbsp granulated sugar
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tbsp shortening, melted
- 1/2 cup chopped pecans
* combine; mix well; set aside | 1-5
--- Batter
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 1/4 cups granulated sugar
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1/4 cup shortening, melted
- 1 cup peeled, grated apple, lightly packed
* combine in a large bowl | 1-7
* beat together | 8-10
* stir in, mixing just until blended | s1 s2
> stir in | 11
> spread evenly in pan
> sprinkle over batter | p1
> {bake}, until a toothpick comes out clean
> serve warm or cool
note: Like a big spicy apple muffin cut into wedges. Mix the batter as little as possible to avoid a coarse, tough texture. Freezes very well.

* beat in a large bowl | 3
> stir in | 4-8
