=== Savannah Pirloo
cat: Fish & Seafood
serves: 4
from: Cha-Bella Restaurant, Savannah
scan: Savannah Pirloo(Shrimp).jpg
--- Risotto
- 4 tbsp butter
- 3/4 cup onion, finely chopped
- 1/8 tsp crushed red pepper
- 1 tsp minced garlic
- 1 1/2 cups Carolina Gold or medium-grain white rice
- 5 cups shrimp stock
- 1/4 cup dry white wine
- salt and pepper, to taste
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
* melt in a large heavy saucepan over medium | 1
> sauté until the onion is pale golden, ~4 min | 2-4
> add; stir to coat, ~2 min | 5
* mix | 6 7
* add 2 cups to the rice; simmer until absorbed, stirring often | s3 s4
> add the rest 1 cup at a time, letting each absorb, ~20 min
> cook until tender and creamy, ~5 min more
> remove from heat; season with | 8 9
--- Seafood
- 1/4 lb wild shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1/4 lb diver scallops
- 4 oz fresh grouper
- salt and pepper
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 1 tsp minced garlic
- 1/8 tsp crushed red pepper
- 1/4 lb fresh mussels
* season | 1-4
* heat in a pan over medium-high | 5
> sear seafood until golden, about 1 min per side; remove | s1
> add; add mussels and simmer until they open | 6-10
--- Vegetables
- 1/2 cup fresh corn
- 1/2 pint grape tomatoes, halved
- Swiss chard, stemmed and coarsely chopped
- extra-virgin olive oil
* sauté in a separate pan until tender | 1-4
> fold in the risotto | p1
> top with shrimp, scallops, grouper and mussels | p2

- pinch ground nutmeg
- 2/3 cup lard
- 1/4 cup ice water
* mix with a fork | 1-5

=== Pumpkin Spice Scones with Cinnamon Icing
cat: Breads & Muffins
serves: 12
from: Canadian Living, May 2006
scan: Scones (Pumpkin,Cherry).jpg
bake: 400F 18-20 sheet
pre: Preheat oven to 400°F
pre: Line a large rimless baking sheet with parchment (or dust with flour)
- 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/3 cup packed brown sugar
- 2 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp each ground ginger, cinnamon and salt
- pinch ground cloves
- 1/2 cup cold butter, cubed
- 3/4 cup buttermilk
- 1/2 cup canned pumpkin purée
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 cup icing sugar
- 2 tbsp milk
- 1/4 tsp cinnamon
* whisk together in a large bowl | 1-6
> cut in until like coarse crumbs | 7
* whisk together | 8-11
* add to flour mixture; stir with a fork to a soft dough | s2 s3
> knead gently 10 times on a floured surface
> pat into a 10 × 7-inch rectangle; trim edges
> cut into 6 squares, then each diagonally in half; place on pan
> {bake} in the centre, until golden; cool on a rack
* whisk, adding up to 1 tsp water to make it spreadable | 12-14
* spread over scones; let set ~1 hr | s8 s9
note: Shapes: for rounds, pat ½ inch thick and cut 2½-inch circles (about 15, bake ~15 min); for wedges, pat into a ½-inch round, cut 8–12 wedges without separating, bake ~30 min. Keeps 24 hours airtight or 2 weeks frozen.
