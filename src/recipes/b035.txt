=== Easy Roast Pork Supper
cat: Pork
serves: 4
from: President's Choice
scan: Roast Pork Super.jpg
pre: Preheat oven to 425°F
- 1 bag (907 g) golden fingerling potatoes
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1 seasoned herb & garlic pork tenderloin
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh basil
- lemon wedges and frozen vegetables, to serve
* toss together in a large roasting pan | 1-4
> roast in the centre 12 min until starting to brown
> push potatoes to the sides; add pork to the centre | 5
> reduce to 400°F; roast 27–29 min, turning pork once, until cooked
> rest pork 5 min; slice 1 inch thick
> serve with potatoes, sprinkled with basil | 6 7

=== Roasted Squash and Garlic Soup with Red Pepper Drizzle
cat: Soups & Stews
serves: about 10 cups
from: Susan Sampson, Toronto Star (adapted from Foodland Ontario)
scan: Roasted Squash and Garlic Soup.jpg
pre: Preheat oven to 375°F
- 1 butternut squash (about 3 lb)
- 3 onions, peeled and quartered
- 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp dried thyme leaves
- 1/2 tsp dried rosemary leaves, crumbled
- 1/4 tsp or more kosher salt
- freshly ground pepper, to taste
- 1 head garlic, separated into cloves but not peeled
- 3 roasted red peppers
- 7 cups chicken or vegetable stock
* peel; seed; cut neck and bulb into 1–1½-inch segments; put in a large bowl | 1
> add; stir gently | 2-7
> spread on a baking sheet
* wrap in a square of foil; place on the sheet | 8
* roast 30 min until garlic is very soft; set garlic aside | s3 s4
> stir squash; roast ~20 min more until very tender and caramelized
* purée in a blender with some of their liquid until thin enough to drizzle | 9
* squeeze garlic pulp into the blender; purée with squash and stock in batches | s6 10
> pour into a large pan; warm over medium; adjust seasoning
* serve drizzled with red pepper purée | s9 s7

=== Bacardi Golden Rum & Nut Cake
cat: Cakes
serves: 12-16
from: Bacardi
scan: Rum and Nut Cake.jpg
bake: 325F 60 bundt-10
pre: Preheat oven to 325°F
pre: Grease and flour a 12-cup Bundt or 10-cup tube pan
--- Cake
- 1 cup chopped pecans or walnuts
- 1 pkg (18½ oz) yellow cake mix
- 1 pkg (3½ oz) vanilla instant pudding mix
- 4 eggs
- 1/2 cup gold rum
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 1/2 cup cold water
* sprinkle over the bottom of the pan | 1
* beat at low until moistened, then high 2 min | 2-7
* pour batter over nuts | s1 s2
> {bake}, until a skewer comes out clean
> cool 15 min; invert onto a serving plate
--- Glaze
- 1/2 stick of butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 1/4 cup water
- 1/2 cup gold rum
* melt in a saucepan | 1
> stir in; boil 5 min, stirring | 2-4
--- Finish
* prick the top; spoon and brush glaze over the top and sides, letting it soak in; repeat until used | p1 p2
note: Decorate the border with whipped cream before serving, if you like.
