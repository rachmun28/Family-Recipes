=== Angel Pie
cat: Pies & Tarts
serves: 8
scan: Angel Pie.jpg
pre: Preheat oven to 275°F
--- Meringue shell
- 4 egg whites
- 1/2 tsp cream of tartar
- 1 cup sugar
* beat until frothy | 1
> beat to points | 2
> add gradually; beat until stiff & glossy | 3
> spread in 9" pie plate
> bake 20 min at 275°F, then 40 min at 300°F
> let cool
--- Lemon filling
- 4 egg yolks
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 3 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tsp grated lemon rind
- 2 tbsp water (only if too thick)
* beat | 1 2
> add | 3-5
> cook in double boiler, stirring, until thick
> let cool
--- Assemble
- 1 cup whipping cream
- toasted coconut, to garnish (optional)
* whip | 1
* spread half the cream over shell, then the custard, then the rest of the cream | p1 p2 s1
> garnish | 2
> let stand 2 hrs before cutting

=== Crunchy Apple Chicken Salad
cat: Salads & Dressings
serves: 6
from: Foodland Ontario
scan: Apple Chicken Salad.jpg
- 3 cups diced cooked chicken or turkey
- 1 cup sliced celery
- 2 cups cored, unpeeled, chopped red-skinned apples (3 to 4 apples)
- 1/4 cup chopped hazelnuts
- 1/4 cup shredded carrot
- 2 tbsp finely chopped green onion
- 1/4 cup light mayonnaise
- 1/4 cup plain yogurt
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- pinch of cinnamon
* mix in a large bowl until combined | 1-6
* stir together in a small bowl | 7-11
* mix together; add more salt & pepper to taste | s1 s2
> refrigerate, covered, until serving
note: Best apples for salads: Cortland. Empire, Golden Delicious, McIntosh, Mutsu (Crispin) and Spartan are good all-rounders.
