=== Canoe's Braised Pork with Steam Whistle Pilsner
cat: Pork
serves: 6
from: Chef Anthony Walsh, Canoe (Toronto Star)
scan: Braised Pork with Pilsner.jpg
--- Marinade
- 1/4 cup kosher salt
- 4 cups fresh apple cider
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 cup diced onion
- 1/2 cup diced carrot
- 1/2 cup diced celery
- 4 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tbsp crushed juniper berries
- 1 sprig each thyme and rosemary
- 1 bottle Steam Whistle Pilsner (or other pilsner)
- 1/2 cup grade A maple syrup
- 3-5 lb bone-in piglet shoulder or bone-in picnic shoulder, skin removed
* dissolve salt in 1 cup of the cider over medium heat; set aside | 1 2
* heat in a large deep skillet over medium | 3
> cook 5 min until soft but not brown | 4-7
> add; cook, stirring, 1 min | 8 9
> add dissolved salt, remaining cider, beer and syrup | s1 10 11
> boil 1 min; strain through a fine sieve; cool completely
* tie with butcher twine if using a picnic shoulder | 12
* put pork in a large non-metallic bowl with marinade; cover & refrigerate 24 hrs, turning | s6 s7
--- Braise
- salt and black pepper to taste
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 sweet onion, thinly sliced
- 3 bay leaves
- 2 sprigs each thyme and rosemary
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 bottle Steam Whistle Pilsner
- 4 cups chicken stock
- yellow split peas, cooked, to serve
* remove pork, discard marinade; pat dry; season | p1 1
* heat 2 tbsp in a large ovenproof pot over medium | 2
> brown pork on all sides; remove to a plate | s1
> add remaining oil; cook 5 min until soft, not brown | 3-5
> add; cook, stirring, 1 min | 6
> add beer; boil over high heat, scraping up brown bits, 3–5 min until reduced by half | 7
> add stock and pork; bring to a boil | 8
> cover tightly; cook in a 350°F oven 3–4 hrs until falling off the bone, turning after 1½ hrs
> remove pork; strain and reserve juices; pull meat into chunks, discarding bones and fat
> drizzle with juices; serve with | 9
