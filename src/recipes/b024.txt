=== Asparagus Gruyère Tart
cat: Appetizers
serves: 12
scan: Mixed Greens with Lemon Chive Dressing.jpg
pre: Preheat oven to 450°F
pre: Line 2 rimless baking sheets with parchment, or grease
- 2 bunches (1 lb each) thin asparagus, trimmed
- 1 pkg (450 g) all-butter puff pastry, thawed
- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 1/2 cups shredded Gruyère cheese
- 1/2 tsp coarsely cracked pepper
- 1 egg
- 1 tbsp milk
* steam until tender-crisp, 3 min; chill in cold water; pat dry | 1
* unroll each sheet onto a pan; spread with mustard, leaving a 1-inch border | 2 3
* arrange asparagus side by side, alternating ends | s2 s1
> sprinkle with | 4 5
* beat together | 6 7
* brush lightly over the pastry border | s4 s5
> bake in the top and bottom thirds, switching halfway, until puffed and golden, 18 min
> cut each into 6 pieces; serve warm or cool
note: Can be set aside for up to 6 hours after baking.

=== Mocha Cheesecake
cat: Desserts
serves: 6-8
from: Great American Recipes
scan: Mocha Cheesecake.jpg
scan: Mocha Cheesecake Microwave.jpg
bake: 350F 40 springform-9
pre: Preheat oven to 350°F
--- Crust
- 1 1/2 cups chocolate wafers, finely crushed
- 1/3 cup butter
- 1/2 tbsp granulated sugar
* combine | 1-3
> butter the sides and bottom of an 8-inch springform; press crumbs evenly onto the bottom
--- Filling
- 2 squares (1 oz each) sweet chocolate
- 2 pkg (8 oz each) cream cheese
- 4 eggs
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 2-3 tsp instant coffee
- dash salt
* melt over hot, not boiling, water; stir smooth | 1
* beat until soft and smooth | 2
> add one at a time | 3
> gradually add, mixing until blended | 4
> add with chocolate; stir until blended | s1 5 6
> turn into the pan | p1
> {bake} in the centre, until almost set (it firms when chilled)
> cool 45 min; cover and chill at least 4 hrs
--- Garnish
- chocolate cookies, mint leaves, raspberries, chocolate leaves
* remove sides of pan; garnish | p2 1
note: Microwave version: melt butter 30–40 s at medium-high, mix with crumbs and sugar and press into an 8-inch microsafe pie dish; microwave on high 1–1½ min. Soften the cream cheese 2 min at medium-high, melt the chocolate 2 min at medium-high, combine everything, pour over the crust and microwave at medium ~14 min until almost set, turning twice. Stand 1 min, cool, chill 4 hrs. (The microwave card uses ½ cup sugar.)
