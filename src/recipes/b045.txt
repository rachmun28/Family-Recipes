=== Teriyaki Pork Chops
cat: Pork
serves: 4
scan: Teriyaki Pork Chops.jpg
pre: Preheat oven to 400°F
- 1/2 cup rice wine vinegar or sake
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 green onions, coarsely chopped
- 2 pieces lemon rind
- 1 clove garlic, coarsely chopped
- 1 piece gingerroot (about 1 inch square)
- 4 boneless pork loin chops (about 4 oz each)
* bring to a boil in a small saucepan; cook ~5 min until reduced by half and thickened | 1-7
* place in a glass baking dish | 8
* pour marinade over; marinate 10 min at room temperature | s1 s2
> trim fat; arrange in one layer on a foil-lined baking sheet (keep the marinade)
> bake at 400°F 10–12 min, brushing twice with marinade, until just a little pink inside
> serve with the pan juices
note: Or grill about 15 min, brushing with sauce every 5 min and turning often.

=== Thai Grilled Chicken
cat: Chicken & Poultry
serves: 4
from: Campbell's
scan: Thai Grilled Chicken.jpg
- 1 1/2 cups reduced-salt chicken broth
- 2 tbsp less-sodium soy sauce
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh cilantro
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger root
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1/4 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
- 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 1 lb)
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
* combine in a resealable bag | 1-8
> add chicken; turn to coat; refrigerate 1 hr, turning | 9
> grill or broil the chicken 15 min until cooked, turning and brushing often with marinade
> mix the remaining marinade with cornstarch in a small saucepan; boil, stirring, until thickened; simmer 5 min; serve with the chicken | 10
note: Marinate asparagus spears in a separate bag and grill until tender-crisp, basting with their own marinade.

=== Tomato and Sweet Basil Sauce
cat: Sauces & Marinades
serves: about 3 cups
from: Elizabeth Baird (Toronto Star)
scan: Tomato Sauce and Variations.jpg
- 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 medium onion, finely diced
- 3 large cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inner stalk celery, finely diced
- 1/2 medium carrot, finely diced
- 1 can (28 oz) tomatoes
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 1/4 cup tomato paste
- 1/4 cup finely chopped fresh basil
- 1/2 tsp granulated sugar
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp butter
* heat in a heavy saucepan over medium | 1
> add; cook, stirring often, ~10 min until softened | 2-5
> add; break into small pieces with a potato masher | 6
> stir in, with half the basil | 7-11
> simmer gently uncovered, stirring often, ~45 min until thick enough to mound on a spoon
> stir in butter; simmer 5 min; stir in the rest of the basil | 12
note: Lovely with seafood such as jumbo shrimp. If you have parsley, add a couple of spoonfuls, finely minced, just before serving.

=== Magnificent Mushroom Sauce
cat: Sauces & Marinades
serves: about 3 1/2 cups
from: Elizabeth Baird (Toronto Star)
scan: Tomato Sauce and Variations.jpg
- 1 pkg (14 g) dried porcini mushrooms
- 1 cup boiling water
- 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 3 large cloves garlic, minced
- 3 cups thinly sliced white button mushrooms (1/2 lb)
- 1/2 tsp crumbled dried oregano or marjoram
- 1/4 cup dry white wine
- 1 can (28 oz) tomatoes
- 1/4 cup tomato paste
- pinch salt
- 1 tsp balsamic vinegar
- 2 tbsp minced fresh flat-leaf parsley
* soak in boiling water 15 min; drain, keeping the liquid; finely dice | 1 2
* heat in a large saucepan over medium | 3
> sauté ~5 min until softened | 4 5
> add porcini, white mushrooms and oregano; raise heat; sauté ~10 min until golden at the edges | s1 6 7
> add; stir and simmer until the wine evaporates | 8
> add; crush with a potato masher, leaving it a little chunky | 9
> stir in the reserved mushroom water | 10 11
> simmer uncovered over very low heat ~30 min until it mounds softly
> stir in vinegar; simmer 5 min; stir in parsley | 12 13

=== Torta Rustica
cat: Breads & Muffins
serves: 6
scan: Torta Rustica.jpg
bake: 350F 40 square-8
--- Dough
- 1/2 cup warm water
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 pkg dry yeast
- 3/4 cup lukewarm milk
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp shortening
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 1/2-3 cups all-purpose flour
* stir sugar into water until dissolved | 1 2
> sprinkle yeast over; stand 10 min; stir well | 3
> stir in, with half the flour; beat well with a wooden spoon | 4-7
> mix in enough of the remaining flour for a soft, easy-to-handle dough | 8
> knead on a floured board until smooth and elastic, ~10 min
> put in a greased bowl; cover with a damp cloth; let rise until nearly double, ~1½ hrs
--- Spinach filling
- 5 oz fresh spinach, washed
- 1 cup ricotta (or cottage cheese)
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 egg yolk
- 1/2 tsp grated onion
- 1/4 tsp pepper
- dash nutmeg
* snip into small pieces with kitchen shears (about 3 cups) | 1
> combine with | 2-7
--- Assemble
- 1 egg yolk
- 1 tbsp water
* punch down dough; divide in two; press one piece into a greased 8-inch square glass pan, 1 inch up the sides | p1
> spread filling evenly over | p2
> roll the rest of the dough into a 9-inch square; cut into 1-inch strips; weave a lattice, tucking the ends under
> cover with a damp cloth; let rise until puffy, ~45 min
* beat together | 1 2
* brush lightly over the lattice | s4 s5
> bake at 350°F until well browned, ~40 min
note: Eat it hot or cold, with a fork or in your hand as a baked sandwich.

=== Café Burgermeister Tourtière
cat: Pork
serves: 2 pies
from: Brent Thrall, Café Burgermeister (Toronto Star)
scan: Tourtiere -Cafe Burgermeister.jpg
- 2 lb ground beef
- 1 1/2 lb ground pork
- 1 1/2 lb ground veal
- 2 cooking onions, finely diced
- 2 cloves garlic, very finely minced
- 4 tbsp chopped parsley
- 1 1/2 tsp savory
- 1 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1 1/2 tsp cloves
- salt and pepper, to taste
- 1 cup water
- pastry for two 8-inch double-crust pies
* cook through in a large pot, stirring often so it doesn't stick — don't overcook or dry it out | 1-11
> spoon the meat and some of the liquid into pie shells | 12
> cover with top crusts; seal; cut steam holes
> bake at 425°F 20 min, then 350°F 25 min until golden brown
> serve with chili sauce

=== Tourtière
cat: Pork
serves: 6
scan: Tourtiere 1.jpg
- 750 g ground lean pork
- 1 small onion, chopped very fine
- 125 mL boiling water
- 1 garlic clove, chopped (or 5 mL garlic powder)
- 1 mL celery salt (or 1 small branch of celery leaves)
- 1 mL pepper
- 7 mL salt
- 1 mL sage
- pinch ground cloves
- 3-4 medium potatoes, boiled and mashed
- pastry for a 2-crust pie, 22 cm diameter
* cook over low heat in a large heavy saucepan, stirring, until the meat is no longer red and about half the liquid is left | 1-9
> cover; cook gently 30–45 min
> mix in; cool | 10
> fill the pie shell; top with pastry | 11
> bake at 230°C (450°F) 10 min, then 180°C (350°F) 30–40 min
