=== Beef Cooking Guide
cat: Kitchen Reference
from: Beef Information Centre
scan: Beef Cooking Chart.jpg
method: Grades: AAA, AA and A are Canada's top grades; the difference is marbling. AAA has the most and is the juiciest and most flavourful. Aging (3–21 days) improves tenderness — look for beef aged at least 10–14 days.
method: Tender roasts (rib, sirloin, tenderloin): place fat side up on a rack in a roasting pan, no lid, no liquid. Season; insert a meat thermometer into the centre (not touching fat or bone). Roast at 325°F (160°C): rare 20 min/lb (45 min/kg), medium 25 min/lb (55 min/kg), well 30 min/lb (65 min/kg).
method: Medium-tender roasts (round, sirloin tip, rump, hip): place fat side up on a rack without lid; season; insert thermometer. Add 1½ cups (375 mL) water to the pan, put the roast in a 500°F (260°C) oven; after 30 min reduce to 275°F (140°C) and cook another 1¼–1½ hours (until medium done) for a 2–5 lb (1–2.4 kg) roast.
method: BBQ roasts: tender cuts — season, insert thermometer, grill fat side up over a drip pan in a closed BBQ over indirect medium heat. Medium-tender cuts — pierce all over with a fork and marinate 12–48 hours in the fridge first; cook only to rare or medium.
method: Pot roasts & stew (cross rib, blade, shoulder, brisket, short ribs, stew cubes): season and coat with flour and seasonings; brown on all sides in a little oil. Add 1–2 cups (250–500 mL) liquid (water, wine, juice, broth or canned soup) for roasts, or 4 cups/lb (1 L/500 g) for stew/short ribs. Cover and simmer on the stove or in a 325°F (160°C) oven until tender — at least 3 hrs for pot roasts, 2 hrs for stews/short ribs. Add vegetables during the last ½ hour.
method: Burgers: combine 1 lb (500 g) ground beef with a beaten egg and ¼ cup (50 mL) bread crumbs or oatmeal if desired. Shape 4–6 even patties ½ inch (1 cm) thick; don't overhandle. Broil, grill or pan-fry until well done (about 5 min per side) — no pink showing, juices clear.
method: Tender steaks (rib-eye, tenderloin, T-bone, wing, striploin, sirloin): season with pepper and garlic; broil or grill on high heat, turning once or twice with tongs. ½–¾ inch thick: rare 3–5, medium 5–7, well 7–9 min per side. 1 inch thick: rare 5–7, medium 7–9, well 9–11 min per side.
method: Medium-tender steaks (round, sirloin tip, blade, cross rib, flank) — broil/BBQ: pierce with a fork, marinate in ½–1 cup liquid 12–24 hrs in the fridge, cook only to rare or medium. Simmer: brown in a lightly oiled pan, add ½–1 cup water, wine, juice, broth or soup, cover and simmer on the stove or in a 325°F (160°C) oven until tender (at least 1½ hours).
