=== Chicken Pot Pie with Phyllo (Cooking Lesson)
cat: Chicken & Poultry
serves: 6
from: Canadian Living, November 1997
scan: Chicken Pot Pie I.jpg
bake: 425F 25-30 casserole-2qt
--- Filling
- 2 cups chicken stock
- 1 cup water
- 1 bay leaf
- 3 lb chicken thighs and breasts, skinned
- 1 potato, peeled and cut in large cubes
- 2 cups cubed peeled butternut squash
- 3 tbsp butter
- 1 cup pearl onions, peeled and halved
- 3 carrots, coarsely chopped
- 3 stalks celery, coarsely chopped
- 2 cups quartered mushrooms
- 3/4 tsp dried thyme
- 3/4 tsp dry mustard
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup 18% cream
- 1 cup frozen peas
* bring to a boil in a large saucepan | 1-3
> add chicken; cover & simmer 15 min (breasts) or 25 min (thighs) until juices run clear | 4
> remove chicken; cool and cut meat into bite-size pieces (set aside)
> return stock to a boil; add; cover & simmer 5 min | 5
> add; cover & cook 10 min until just tender | 6
> discard bay leaf; drain, reserving 2¼ cups liquid
* melt in another saucepan over medium | 7
> cook 10 min, stirring, until softened and starting to brown | 8-11
> add; cook 1 min | 12-16
> sprinkle with flour; cook, stirring, 1 min | 17
> gradually whisk in the reserved liquid and cream; boil, then simmer 5 min until thickened | s6 18
> stir in chicken, potato mixture and peas | 19
> pour into an 8-cup oval casserole
--- Phyllo top
- 5 sheets phyllo pastry
- 1/4 cup butter, melted
* one sheet at a time, brush lightly with butter; scrunch into a 6 × 5-inch oval | 1 2
--- Bake
* place phyllo on filling to cover completely | p1 p2
> bake at 425°F 25–30 min until bubbling and golden
> cool on a rack 5 min before serving

=== Chicken Pot Pie with Filo (Light)
cat: Chicken & Poultry
serves: 4
scan: Chicken Pot Pie.jpg
pre: Preheat oven to 375°F
- 1 cup reduced-sodium chicken broth, defatted
- 3 carrots, in 1/2-inch chunks
- 1 all-purpose potato, peeled, in 1/2-inch chunks
- 1 parsnip, in 1/2-inch chunks
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1 cup frozen pearl onions
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 1 lb skinless boneless chicken thighs, in 2-inch pieces
- 1/2 cup evaporated skimmed milk
- 1/4 cup flour
- 1/2 tsp dried sage
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 2 sheets filo dough (17 × 11 inch), cut crosswise in half
- nonstick cooking spray
* combine in a large saucepan; boil, then cover & simmer ~5 min until potato is almost tender | 1-5
> stir in; return to a boil; cover & simmer ~5 min until chicken is cooked | 6-8
* combine in another saucepan; boil, then simmer ~2 min until like heavy cream | 9-13
> stir in chicken mixture; cook 1 min | s2
> spoon into a 9-inch deep-dish pie pan
> layer filo on top at right angles, tucking in edges and spraying each layer | 14 15
> cut a 3-inch X in the centre; pull back the corners and spray them
> bake at 375°F 10–15 min, until filo is crisp and golden
note: Serve with a watercress and endive salad with citrus vinaigrette.
