=== Potato Bread
cat: Breads & Muffins
serves: 1 loaf (8½ × 4½-inch)
scan: Potato Bread.jpg
bake: 375F 35-40 loaf-8
- 2 medium potatoes, peeled (1/4 kg)
- 1 tsp dry yeast
- pinch sugar
- 1/4 cup warm water
- 1 cup potato water and milk
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp soft butter or vegetable oil
- 1 tbsp sugar (optional)
- 3 1/2 cups all-purpose or bread flour
* cook covered in water until soft; drain, keeping the water; mash and cool slightly | 1
* dissolve until bubbling, ~5 min | 2-4
* mix in with half the flour, then the yeast | s1 5-9 s2
> knead in the rest of the flour until smooth and elastic, ~8 min
> turn in an oiled bowl; enclose loosely in a plastic bag; let double in a warm place
> punch down; shape a loaf in a buttered pan; sprinkle with flour
> cover loosely; let double (1½ inches above the rim)
> {bake}
> remove from pan; cool on a rack
note: Potato bread uses less yeast because the potatoes increase the yeast's strength.

=== Prime Rib Roast
cat: Beef
serves: 8
scan: Prime Rib Roast.jpg
scan: Prime Rib.jpg
pre: Preheat oven to 450°F
- 2 heads freshly roasted garlic
- 1 tbsp sea salt
- 2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 2 tbsp finely chopped fresh rosemary
- 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 prime rib roast (about 6½ lb / 3 kg)
- 1 cup dry red wine
- 1 cup beef stock
- 1 cup dry red wine
- 1 cup beef stock
* mash with a fork until creamy | 1
> stir in to make a paste | 2-6
> rub over the roast; place in a roasting pan | 7
> add to the pan | 8 9
> roast 20 min, then 350°F ~22 min per lb for medium (140–145°F inside); add stock if the pan dries
> remove roast; tent with foil; rest 10 min (meat sits on the board)
> drain excess fat; set the pan on a burner
> add; bring to a boil, scraping up the bits | 10 11
> boil 5 min; season; strain and skim the jus
note: Chef's tips: bring the meat to room temperature first; season just before it goes in a preheated oven; rest at least 15 minutes; carve across the grain. Red Grill's plain version: 450°F for 20 min, then 350°F about 20 min per lb for medium-rare.
