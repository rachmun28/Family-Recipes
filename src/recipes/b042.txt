=== California Rolls
cat: Fish & Seafood
serves: 6 rolls (48 pieces)
scan: Sushi California Rolls.jpg
--- Sushi rice
- 4 cups short-grain rice
- 4 1/3 cups water
- 1/2 cup white vinegar
- 3 tbsp sugar
- 1 1/2 tbsp salt
- pinch Ajinomoto (Accent), optional
* wash and drain until the water runs clear | 1
> add; let sit 1 hr | 2
> cook over medium 10 min, gradually bringing to a boil, then lower to keep it from bubbling over; boil 10 min
> turn heat to high for 5 seconds; turn off; stand 10 min with the lid tightly closed
* mix ahead of time to make the dressing | 3-6
* sprinkle over the hot rice; mix together quickly | s4 s5
--- Rolls
- 6 sheets nori, 1/3 cut off
- goma (roasted sesame seed)
- tobiko (fish roe) or lumpfish caviar
- cucumber
- avocado
- kani-kama (imitation crab, leg style)
* place nori shiny side down on the roller | 1
> spread rice to the edges; sprinkle with sesame | p1 2
> press plastic wrap over the rice; flip to the nori side
> lay along the centre | 3-6
> roll tightly with the plastic wrap
> cut each roll into 8 pieces with the wrap still on
note: Keep your fingers moist with equal parts vinegar and water when spreading rice. Keep rolls in a cool place, not the fridge.

=== Sticky Sushi Rice
cat: Pasta & Rice
serves: about 4 rolls
scan: Sushi Rice 2.jpg
- 1 cup water
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 cups raw short- or medium-grain rice
- 2 cups + 2 tbsp water
- 4 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp + 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt
* mix to make tezu (hand water); set aside | 1-3
* wash several times until clear; drain in a colander 1 hr | 4
> put in a heavy pot or rice cooker with the water; cover tightly; bring to a boil | 5
> simmer covered over low 15 min
> off the heat, stretch a clean tea towel under the lid; let steam 15 min more
* heat in a small saucepan, stirring, until the sugar dissolves; cool | 6-8
* wipe a wide bowl (ideally a wooden hangiri) with tezu; add the hot rice and seasoning, cutting and folding — don't stir | s1 s5 s6
> fan while mixing ~10 min until cool and glossy
> keep covered with a cloth at room temperature; use within an hour (don't refrigerate)
