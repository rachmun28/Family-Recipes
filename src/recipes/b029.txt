=== Peanut Butter Pie (Peanut Advisory Board)
cat: Pies & Tarts
serves: 8
from: Peanut Advisory Board & Hershey
scan: Peanut Butter Pie-Peanut Advisory Board.jpg
--- Vanilla wafer crust
- 1 1/4 cups vanilla wafer crumbs
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1/3 cup melted margarine
* combine until blended | 1-3
> press onto the bottom and sides of an 8-inch pie pan; chill
--- Filling
- 4 oz cream cheese, softened
- 1/2 cup plus 1 tbsp creamy peanut butter
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 3/4 cups nondairy whipped topping, thawed
- 1/3 cup chopped peanut butter cups
* beat until fluffy; scrape bowl | 1-4
> gently fold in half the topping, then the rest with the peanut butter cups | 5 6
> spread into crust; cover; refrigerate 30 min | p1
--- Chocolate icing
- 1/4 cup milk chocolate chips
- 1 tbsp plus 1 tsp semi-sweet chocolate chips
- 2 tbsp heavy whipping cream
* microwave on high 40–50 s; stir (heat 10–15 s more at a time if needed) | 1-3
--- Peanut butter drizzle
- 1 tbsp creamy peanut butter
- 1 tsp vegetable oil
* microwave on high 10–15 s; stir | 1 2
--- Finish
- 1/4 cup chopped dry-roasted peanuts
- remaining chopped peanut butter cups
* pour icing over the pie from the middle; spread to coat | p2 p3
> pipe a crisscross of drizzle from a bag with a snipped corner | p4
> garnish the rim | 1 2
> refrigerate until firm, ~30 min
note: Best cut frozen.
