=== Oatmeal Bread
cat: Breads & Muffins
serves: 1 loaf (8½ × 4½-inch)
scan: Oatmeal Bread.jpg
bake: 375F 40-45 loaf-8
- 2 1/2 tsp dry yeast (1 pkg)
- pinch sugar
- 1/4 cup warm water
- 1 1/2 cups rolled oats
- 1 1/2 cups hot water
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp oil or soft butter
- 2 tbsp brown sugar (optional)
- 1 cup whole wheat flour
- 2 1/4 cups all-purpose or bread flour
* dissolve until foamy, ~5 min | 1-3
* soak until lukewarm | 4 5
* mix in with part of the white flour | s1 s2 6-9 10
> knead in the rest of the flour until smooth and elastic, ~8 min
> turn in a buttered bowl; enclose in a plastic bag; let rise until double
> punch down (let rise again if you like)
> knead slightly; shape into a loaf in the pan
> cover loosely; let rise until 1½ inches above the rim
> {bake}
> remove from pan; cool on a rack
