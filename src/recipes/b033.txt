=== Pumpkin Gnocchi with Sage
cat: Pasta & Rice
serves: 6
from: Chatelaine
scan: Pumpkin Gnocchi with Sage.jpg
- 1 large white potato
- 1 cup pure pumpkin purée
- 1 egg yolk
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp ground ginger
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 3 tbsp butter
- 8 large fresh sage leaves
* boil unpeeled, partly covered, until fork-tender, 25–35 min; chill just until cool enough to peel | 1
> peel; rice or mash; measure 1 cup lightly packed
> stir together until combined | 2-6
> gradually stir in 1¼ cups flour until smooth and barely sticky (add more 1 tbsp at a time if needed) | 7
> knead a couple of times; divide in 3; roll each into a 5 × 7-inch rectangle ½ inch thick
> cut into 1 × ½-inch pieces
> cook in boiling water in two batches until they float, 2–3 min; keep warm
* melt in the same pot over medium-high until sizzling | 8
> tear in; stir until fragrant, ~1 min | 9
* pour over gnocchi; toss gently | s7 s9
note: Roast a small pumpkin covered with foil for 1 hour at 400°F and purée the pulp instead of canned. A ricer gives fluffier gnocchi.
