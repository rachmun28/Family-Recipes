=== Buttercream Icing
cat: Icings & Sauces
serves: 3 cups
from: Wilton
scan: Icing recipes.jpg
- 1/2 cup solid vegetable shortening
- 1/2 cup butter or margarine
- 1 tsp clear vanilla extract
- 4 cups sifted icing sugar (about 1 lb)
- 2 tbsp milk
* cream with an electric mixer | 1 2
> add | 3
> add 1 cup at a time, beating well on medium; scrape the bowl often | 4
> add; beat at medium until light and fluffy | 5
note: For pure white, stiffer icing use all shortening plus ½ tsp butter flavouring. To thin for icing a cake, add 2 more tbsp milk or 3–4 tbsp light corn syrup. Keeps 2 weeks refrigerated in an airtight container; rewhip before using.
note: Chocolate buttercream: add ¾ cup cocoa (or three 1-oz squares unsweetened chocolate, melted) and 1–2 tbsp more milk.

from: Wilton
scan: Icing recipes.jpg

- 4 tsp cold water
- 1 cup heavy whipping cream, very cold
- 1/4 cup icing sugar
- 1/2 tsp clear vanilla extract
* combine in a small saucepan; let stand until thick | 1 2
> stir over low heat just until dissolved; cool slightly
* whip until slightly thickened | 3-5
* while beating slowly, add gelatin gradually; whip at high until stiff | s2 s3
note: Cakes iced with whipped cream must be kept in the refrigerator.
