=== Easy Asian Grilled Beef Steak
cat: Beef
serves: 4-6
scan: Asian Grilled Beef Steak.jpg
- 3/4 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup packed brown sugar
- 1 tbsp minced ginger root
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- dash of sesame oil (optional)
- 4 strip loin grilling steaks (2 lb total, about 1 inch thick)
* combine in a large sealable freezer bag | 1-6
* pierce all over with a fork | 7
* add steaks to marinade; marinate in the fridge 30 min–2 hrs | s1 s2
> remove steaks (discard marinade); pat dry
> grill over medium-high heat (400°F) 5–6 min per side for medium (160°F inside)
> cover loosely with foil; rest 5–10 min
note: To use the marinade as a dipping sauce, boil it for 1 minute first. Steaks keep cooking after they come off the heat: final temperature is 160°F (71°C) for medium and 170°F (77°C) for well done.
