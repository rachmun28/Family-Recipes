=== Eggnog Cheesecake
cat: Desserts
serves: 12
scan: Eggnog Cheesecake.jpg
pre: Preheat oven to 325°F
--- Crust
- 1 cup graham cracker crumbs
- 2 tbsp granulated sugar
- 3 tbsp melted butter or margarine
* combine | 1-3
> press onto the bottom of a 9-inch springform pan
> bake at 325°F 10 min; cool on a rack
> raise oven to 425°F
--- Filling
- 1 1/2 lb cream cheese
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup whipping cream
- 3 eggs
- 2 tbsp rum (or 2 tbsp orange juice + 1 tsp rum extract)
- 1/2 tsp nutmeg
* blend until smooth in a processor or mixer | 1-4
> add; blend | 5-7
> pour into crust | p1
> bake at 425°F 10 min, then 250°F ~45 min until the centre is barely firm
> loosen from the rim at once; cool completely before removing the sides
--- Chocolate holly garnish
- 2 oz semi-sweet chocolate
- holly leaves
- small round red candies
- gold or silver dragées
* melt over hot, not boiling, water | 1
> paint onto the shiny side of the leaves; freeze 5 min on a sheet | 2
> peel off the leaves; refrigerate until firm, ~15 min
> pipe holly stems with leftover chocolate; arrange leaves, candies and dragées as berries | p2 3 4
note: Instead of real leaves, spread the chocolate ⅛ inch thick between waxed paper, chill until firm but not hard, and cut holly shapes with cookie cutters.
