=== Sweet & Sour Pork Ribs (Slow Cooker)
cat: Pork
serves: 6
scan: Sweet and Sour Pork Ribs.jpg
- 3 lb pork side ribs
- 2 cups brown sugar, packed
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 can (14 oz) pineapple tidbits in juice, undrained
- 2 tbsp each soy sauce and ketchup
- 1/2 cup white vinegar
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 tsp ginger, grated
* cut into 2–3 rib sections | 1
* mix in a small saucepan; heat and stir over medium-high until boiling and thickened | 2-8
* layer ribs in a slow cooker, spooning sauce over each layer | s1 s2
> cover; cook on low 10–12 hrs (or high 5–6) until very tender
note: To thicken the sauce afterwards, pour the juice into a saucepan, add 2 tbsp cornstarch dissolved in ¼ cup water and bring to a boil.
