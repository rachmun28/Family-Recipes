=== Key Lime Pie (Light)
cat: Pies & Tarts
serves: 8
from: Prevention
scan: Key Lime Pie.jpg
pre: Preheat oven to 375°F
pre: Coat a 9-inch pie plate with cooking spray
--- Crust
- 1 egg white, lightly beaten
- 1 1/2 cups crumbled gingersnaps
- 1 1/2 tbsp butter or margarine, melted
- 1 1/2 tbsp canola oil
* mix | 1-4
> press over the bottom and up the sides of the pie plate
> bake 8–10 min until lightly browned; cool on a rack
--- Filling
- 1/2 cup Key lime juice
- 1 tbsp grated lime rind
- 1 can (14 oz) nonfat sweetened condensed milk
- 2 egg yolks
- 4 egg whites
- 1/4 tsp cream of tartar
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 tsp cornstarch
* mix | 1 2
> whisk in | 3 4
* beat until foamy | 5 6
* combine | 7 8
* gradually beat into whites until stiff peaks | s3 s4
* fold ¾ cup of the whites into lime mixture | s2 s5
> pour into crust; bake 15 min | p1
> spread the rest of the whites over the filling to the edges
> bake 10 min until golden and set
> cool completely; refrigerate 1–2 hrs
note: Bottled Key lime juice, or regular lime juice, works fine.
