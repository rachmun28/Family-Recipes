=== Better Biscuits
cat: Breads & Muffins
serves: 13
from: Best American Recipes 2005–2006 (Toronto Star)
scan: Tea Biscuits (Toronto Star).jpg
bake: 425F 15 sheet
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tbsp granulated sugar
- 2 1/4 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp cream of tartar
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 1/4 tsp sea salt
- 1/4 cup each shortening and butter, in small pieces
- 3/4 cup buttermilk
- whole milk, for brushing
* whisk together in a large bowl | 1-6
> cut in with a pastry blender until like small bits and flakes | 7
> pour over; stir with a wooden spoon just until it comes together but is slightly moist | 8
> knead lightly in the bowl 3 or 4 times
> roll or pat into a 1-inch-thick round; cut with a floured 2-inch cutter; place 2 inches apart on an ungreased sheet
> press scraps together and cut a few more — don't overwork
> preheat oven to 400°F; brush tops with milk (don't let it drip down the sides) | 9
> put in the oven and turn it up to 425°F right away; bake until golden and high, ~15 min
> cool on a rack 2 min
note: Overworked dough makes tough biscuits, and milk dripping down the sides stops them rising.
