=== Bluestone Roasted Butternut Squash Soup
cat: Soups & Stews
serves: 4-6
from: Bluestone Bistro
scan: Soup-Roasted Butternut Squash.jpg
pre: Preheat oven to 375°F
- 1 large butternut squash (or 2 small)
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- salt and pepper
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1/2-3/4 cup onion, very finely diced
- 2-3 cups chicken stock
- 1/4 cup maple syrup
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- cinnamon, to taste (about 1/4–1/2 tsp)
- nutmeg, to taste (about 1/8–1/4 tsp)
- 1/2-1 cup 10% or 35% cream
* halve lengthwise; seed | 1
> brush with oil; season; bake cut side down 45–60 min until tender | 2 3
* melt in a large saucepan | 4
> cook until softened; set aside | 5
* scoop squash into the pan, discarding skin | s2 s4
> add stock to cover by about an inch; boil, then simmer ~20 min | 6
> add | 7-11
> purée until smooth (freeze or refrigerate at this point if you like)
> add as much cream as you like; warm over low — don't boil | 12
note: 10% cream makes a thinner soup, 35% a richer one. If reheating, warm first, then add the cream and a little more stock if needed.

scan: Speedy Pat in Pastry.jpg

- 1 1/2 tsp sugar
- 3/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup oil
- 3 tbsp cold milk
* sift into a 9-inch pie plate | 1-3
* beat till creamy | 4 5

=== Spice Cake with Broiled Topping

* cream | 7 8
> beat in one at a time | 9
* add dry ingredients alternately with buttermilk | s3 s1 10
> turn into the pan; {bake}
--- Broiled topping

- 2 tbsp flour
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup melted butter

=== Spicy Pumpkin Loaf

- 1/2 cup raisins
- 1/2 cup chopped walnuts
* sift together | 1-8
* beat until light | 9
> add; beat till thoroughly combined | 10-13
* add liquid all at once to a well in the dry ingredients; stir until well mixed | s1 s3
> add | 14 15
> {bake}
> remove from pan after 10 min; peel off paper; cool completely on a rack
> wrap closely; store at least 8 hrs (it slices more easily)
--- Glaze (optional)

- 1/4 cup water
* bring just to a rolling boil | 1 2
> brush over the loaf before serving | p1
