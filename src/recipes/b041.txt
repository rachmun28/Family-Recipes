=== Butternut Squash Soup (The National Soup)
cat: Soups & Stews
serves: 6
from: Elizabeth Baird (Toronto Star)
scan: Squash Soup, Pasta, Sides.jpg
- 2 tbsp butter or canola oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 2 large cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 tbsp grated or diced gingerroot
- 1 tbsp mild curry paste or powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp freshly ground pepper
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 5 cups cubed peeled butternut squash
- 1 large potato, peeled and cubed
- 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth, sodium reduced
- 1/3 cup light cream (5%, 10% or 18%)
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
* melt in a Dutch oven over medium | 1
> fry, stirring, ~5 min until the onion is tender | 2-7
> add; cook, stirring, ~1 min until slightly darkened | 8
> add; stir to coat with the curry mixture | 9 10
> stir in, scraping up brown bits | 11
> bring to a boil; cover and simmer ~35 min until very tender
> purée (make-ahead: cool, refrigerate up to 3 days)
> add; heat through until steaming | 12 13
note: Serve with a swirl of sour cream or yogurt, toasted slivered almonds or finely diced red pepper. For a vegan version use oil, vegetable broth and more broth in place of the cream.
