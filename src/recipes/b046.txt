=== Traditional Yule Log
cat: Desserts
serves: 12-14
from: Monarch
scan: Traditional Yule Log.jpg
bake: 350F 18-20 jelly-15x10
pre: Preheat oven to 350°F
pre: Grease a 15 × 10-inch jelly roll pan and line with foil
- 1/4 cup butter
- 1 cup chopped pecans or walnuts
- 1 cup flaked coconut
- 1 can (300 mL) sweetened condensed milk
- 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 4 eggs
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 2 tbsp water
- 3 squares (3 oz) unsweetened chocolate, melted
- icing sugar, for the towel
- chocolate icing
* melt in the pan | 1
> sprinkle evenly with nuts and coconut | 2 3
> drizzle evenly over the top | 4
* combine | 5-7
* beat on high until light coloured, ~5 min | 8
> gradually beat in | 9
> blend in | 10 11
> fold in gently but thoroughly | s4
* spread over the condensed milk | s3 s8
> {bake}, until the top springs back
* sprinkle a tea towel with sifted icing sugar | 12
* loosen the edges; turn out onto the towel; remove the foil and pan | s10 s11
> from the short side, roll up with the nut layer inside (don't roll the towel in); wrap the towel firmly around it until cool
> trim the ends; use slices for log bumps
> frost completely; score with a fork to look like bark | 13
> garnish as desired

=== Oatmeal Casserole Bread
cat: Breads & Muffins
serves: 1 round loaf
from: Monarch
scan: Traditional Yule Log.jpg
- 1 1/2 cups boiling water
- 1 cup rolled oats
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1/4 cup molasses
- 3 tbsp oil
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1/3 cup warm water
- 1 pkg active dry yeast
- 4 cups all-purpose flour
* pour over oats; mix well | 1 2
> stir in; cool to lukewarm | 3-6
* dissolve sugar in water; sprinkle in yeast; stand 10 min; stir | 7-9
* add, with 1½ cups of the flour; beat on low until blended, then high 1½–2 min | s2 s3 10
> stir in the rest of the flour by hand (dough is sticky)
> cover with a tea towel; let rise until doubled, 45–60 min
> stir down; place in a well-greased and floured 2-quart casserole
> cover; let rise until almost doubled and the top has mushroomed, 1–1½ hrs
> bake on the bottom rack at 375°F 35–40 min, covering with foil for the last 10 min
> remove from the dish; cool on a rack
note: A lovely gift: wash the casserole, return the cooled bread to it, tie with ribbon and add festive oven mitts.

=== Lazy Lemonade Turkey Kabobs

serves: 4
scan: Turkey Kabobs.jpg

- 1 ripe but firm mango, peeled, in 1½-inch chunks
- 1/4 large honeydew melon, peeled, in 1½-inch chunks
- vegetable oil cooking spray

- 2 cups peeled, seeded, chopped cucumber
- 1 cup chopped or sliced red onion
- 1 large clove garlic, crushed
- 1/2 cup sliced fresh basil leaves
- 1/3 cup kalamata olives
* whisk together | 1-3
> stir in | 4
* mix in a large bowl | 5-11
* add dressing; toss well; season to taste | s3 s2
> serve immediately
note: Greek: add 2 tbsp chopped fresh oregano, 2 tbsp mint and ¾ cup cubed feta. Middle Eastern: skip the vinegar and use 3 tbsp lemon juice; use torn pita and mint or cilantro instead of basil; skip the olives; add 1 cup chickpeas if you like.
