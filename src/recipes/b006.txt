=== The Ultimate Butter Tart
cat: Pies & Tarts
serves: about 12 tarts
from: Sun-Maid / Crisco
scan: Butter Tart-Ultimate.jpg
bake: 425F 12-15 muffin*12
pre: Preheat oven to 425°F
- pastry for a double-crust pie
- 1/2 cup lightly packed brown sugar
- 1/2 cup corn syrup
- 1/4 cup shortening
- 1 egg, slightly beaten
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 3/4 cup raisins
* roll out thinly; cut 4-inch rounds; fit into medium muffin cups | 1
> divide raisins among the shells | 8
* combine; mix well | 2-7
* fill shells ⅔ full with syrup mixture | s2 s3
> {bake} on the bottom shelf, just until set — don't overbake
> cool on a wire rack, then remove from pans
note: If you like runny, gooey tarts you'll love these — underbaking makes them runnier. Try them warm with ice cream.
