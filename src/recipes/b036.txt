serves: 12
scan: S'mores Cheesecake.jpg

- 2 1/4 cups graham wafer crumbs

> press into the bottom of a 10-inch springform
--- Filling

- 1 can sweetened condensed milk
- 2 tsp vanilla

- 1 cup miniature marshmallows
* beat until smooth | 1-3
> add; beat until combined | 4
> stir in | 5 6
> pour over crust | p1
> {bake}, until the centre is almost set
--- Topping
- 1 cup miniature marshmallows
- 1/2 cup chocolate chips

* melt together | 2 3
* drizzle over the marshmallows | s1 s2
