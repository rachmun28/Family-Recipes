- 1/4 cup finely chopped fresh rosemary
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp finely chopped fresh thyme

- 1 beef rib roast or boneless ribeye roast (4–6 lb)

> mix in | 3-7

=== Ribs in Barbecue Sauce
cat: Pork
serves: 6
from: Margaret Howard, All Fired Up! (Toronto Star)
scan: Ribs in Barbeque Sauce.jpg
- 4 lb side or back ribs
- 1 cup ketchup or chili sauce
- 1/3 cup cider vinegar
- 1/3 cup brown sugar, lightly packed
- 2 tsp mustard
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1/8 tsp each salt, hot pepper sauce and ground pepper
* cover with water in a shallow pot; simmer ~45 min until tender; drain | 1
* combine in a saucepan; simmer uncovered 20 min, stirring (makes ~1½ cups) | 2-10
* pour sauce over ribs; cover and refrigerate several hours | s1 s2
> remove ribs (keep the sauce); grill covered over medium ~20 min, turning and brushing with sauce once
> cover with foil 10 min before serving
note: Don't skip the simmering or the texture will disappoint. Or bake at 350°F for 30 min until heated through. Serve with potato salad or baked potatoes and a cabbage or green salad.
