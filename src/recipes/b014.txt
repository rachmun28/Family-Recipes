=== Dark Chocolate-Orange Biscotti
cat: Cookies
serves: 40-45 cookies
from: Toronto Star (adapted from Martha Stewart Living Cookbook)
scan: Dark Chocolate Orange Biscotti.jpg
pre: Line a 12 × 18-inch baking sheet with parchment
pre: Preheat oven to 350°F
- 6 oz semi-sweet chocolate
- 4 oz semi-sweet chocolate
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter
- 2 cups all-purpose flour, sifted
- 1/2 cup cocoa powder, sifted
- 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 1 cup sugar
- finely grated zest of 1 orange
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 large egg white, lightly beaten
* chop into small chunks; set aside | 1
* melt in a double boiler, stirring until smooth | 2 3
* combine; set aside | 4-7
* beat with a paddle on medium-high until pale and thick, ~2 min | 8-10
> on low, add | 11
> slowly add melted chocolate until combined | s2
> slowly add dry mixture until just combined | s3
> fold in chunks | s1
> cover; rest 10 min

> flatten to 2½ inches wide; brush with | 12

> lower oven to 300°F; cool logs 15 min

=== Chocolate Cream-Filled Easter Eggs
cat: Candy & Fudge
serves: 10-15 eggs
from: Toronto Star, Fare Exchange (Kate Priestman)
scan: Easter Eggs -Cream Filled.jpg
--- Filling
- 150 mL sweetened condensed milk
- 1/2 cup butter, softened
- 1 1/2 tsp corn syrup
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 6-7 cups sifted icing sugar
- yellow food colouring
* mix in a large bowl | 1-5
> add icing sugar in 3 or 4 batches, stirring well; knead until smooth and holds its shape | 6
* colour ¼ of the mixture yellow and form 10–15 small balls | s2 7
> make 10–15 larger balls with the rest; flatten each and wrap around a yolk ball into an egg shape
> cover well with plastic; chill at least 4 hrs until firm
--- Dipping chocolate
- 1 lb semi-sweet chocolate
- 1 oz paraffin wax (optional)
* melt over hot (not boiling) water, stirring, until it reaches 86°F; stir a few minutes more | 1 2
> dip eggs with a fondue fork; tap off excess; set on waxed paper until firm | p1
--- Ornamental icing
- 1/2 lb icing sugar (2 cups)
- 1/4 tsp cream of tartar
- whites of 2 small eggs
- 1/4 tsp vanilla
- food colouring
* beat in a mixer until very stiff | 1-4
> tint as desired; keep covered with a damp cloth | 5
> decorate eggs | p2
note: Use sweetened condensed milk, not evaporated. Keep the chocolate at 86°F — rewarm it over warm water if it cools too much.
